When generating layouts for the Simaskansa PKL Management System, always maintain a balance between "Corporate Cleanliness" and "Academic Friendliness". 

1. Avoid pitch-black colors for text; always use the Deep Navy (#0A1C40).
2. Keep white spaces generous. Do not cramp tables or forms. Use 24px padding for main sections.
3. Use the paper airplane element or subtle dot-grid vectors as decorative accents on empty states or dashboard banners to keep the identity alive across the system.
